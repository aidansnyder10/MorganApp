Hotlines & text lines

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (e.g. 911 in the U.S.).

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233 24/7 · thehotline.org
  • RAINN — sexual assault 1-800-656-HOPE (4673) 24/7 · rainn.org
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 988 (call or text) U.S. · 988lifeline.org
  • Crisis Text Line Text HOME to 741741 U.S. & Canada · crisistextline.org
  • National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-TLC-MAMA (852-6262) 24/7 · English & Spanish · U.S.
  • Postpartum Support International 1-800-944-4773 (call or text “Help”) perinatal mental health · postpartum.net

Phone numbers and websites were accurate to common public listings when this page was built; carriers and organizations may change them. If a link fails, search the organization’s name directly.

Morgan does not operate hotlines and is not affiliated with the organizations listed.
